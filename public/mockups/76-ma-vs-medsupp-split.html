How it works
Margaret, 67 · healthy, on a budget

Sees local in-network doctors. Picks a $0 plan with dental and a gym membership, pays small copays as she goes, and the yearly max caps her risk.

How it works
Robert, 72 · manages conditions, travels

Wants any specialist with no referrals. Picks Plan G plus a Part D plan. Higher premium, but almost nothing out of pocket when he gets care.

Questions to ask before deciding
Do you want to keep specific doctors? Are they in-network?
Do you travel or live part of the year in another state?
How is your health, and any procedures on the horizon?
Low monthly bill with copays, or higher premium with few surprises?
How much do dental, vision, and other extras matter?
What is comfortable for you to pay each month?
