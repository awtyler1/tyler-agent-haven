2027 CMS Final Rule: what actually changes for you

By Andrew Horn, Tyler Insurance Group
1

Marketing & call documentation gets stricter.

2

Plans can't churn clients mid-year as easily.

3

Lead-vendor crackdown favors direct FMO ties.

📋What happened

CMS released its 2027 final rule. The parts that touch you:

  • Stricter documentation on marketing and enrollment calls.
  • New limits on mid-year plan changes.
  • More scrutiny on lead vendors and third-party marketers.
💡Why it matters to you

This raises the compliance floor. High-volume, low-touch shops get squeezed; relationship-driven advisors get a cleaner field — and clients who are harder for outside marketers to poach. Net-net: good for the way you already work.

✅What to do now
  1. Confirm every appointment has a documented SOA on file.
  2. Audit your lead sources and drop any that can't prove compliance.
  3. Work plan stability into your renewal conversations this fall.
