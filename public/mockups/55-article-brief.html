2027 CMS Final Rule: what actually changes for you

By Andrew Horn, Tyler Insurance Group
⭐ Key takeaways
  • ✓ Marketing rules tightened — your SOAs and call recordings matter more than ever.
  • ✓ Plan-stability rules mean fewer mid-year disruptions for your clients.
  • ✓ Tighter oversight on third-party marketers shifts value toward direct FMO relationships — good for you.

CMS released its 2027 final rule last week. Most of it is inside baseball — but three pieces will touch how you sell this AEP. Here's the short version, in plain English.

What changed

  • Stricter documentation around marketing and enrollment calls.
  • New guardrails that limit how often plans can change mid-year.
  • More scrutiny on lead vendors and third-party marketing orgs.

If you're compliant and relationship-driven, this rule works in your favor.

💼 What this means for your business

The agents who lose under this rule are the ones cutting corners on compliance or leaning on sketchy lead vendors. If that's not you, you just got a cleaner playing field — and clients who are less likely to be churned out from under you.

✅ Do this now
  • 1 Make sure every appointment has a documented SOA on file.
  • 2 Review your lead sources — drop any that can't show compliance.
  • 3 Tell renewing clients their plan is more stable in 2027. It's a retention talking point.
