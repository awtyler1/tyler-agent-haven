2027 CMS Final Rule: what actually changes for you

By Andrew Horn, Tyler Insurance Group
The bottom line

If you sell compliant and lead with relationships, the 2027 rule helps you — it squeezes the corner-cutters and makes your renewals stickier.

CMS released its 2027 final rule last week. Most of it won't touch your day-to-day, but three pieces will shape how you sell this AEP.

What changed

  • Stricter documentation on marketing and enrollment calls.
  • New limits on how often plans can change mid-year.
  • More scrutiny on lead vendors and third-party marketers.

Why it matters

The rule raises the floor on compliance. That's bad news for high-volume, low-touch shops — and good news for advisors who already do it right. Your clients are also less likely to get churned by aggressive outside marketers.

Key points
  • ✓ SOAs & call records matter more
  • ✓ Fewer mid-year plan disruptions
  • ✓ Lead-vendor crackdown favors FMO ties
Your move
  • 1 Document an SOA for every appointment
  • 2 Audit your lead sources
  • 3 Use plan stability as a renewal talking point
